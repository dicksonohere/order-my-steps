Priority

A task can carry a letter — A, B, C or D — and a task without one is not lesser. On its own the letter does nothing but sit beside the line and be read.

The chime

A chime closes a prayer that runs its course. A desk with someone in front of you and a room with the door shut want opposite things from it, so where it sits is yours.

How many

One is easily missed by a man who is really praying. Five is not an alarm — it is a sound long enough to reach someone who is not waiting for it.

Set it by ear, at both ends — once with music playing, once in silence.

This can take the chime down. It can never take it above what your phone allows, and on silent nothing sounds at all.

A sleeping phone switches this app off, and a switched-off app cannot sound. So the screen is held awake while a prayer runs — only while it runs. Lock the phone yourself and you have overruled that: the chime waits, and lands when you pick it up.

A time on a task
