Proverbs 3:5–6
God is listening

The gate

Before a day you have not written on, the gate stands. It holds, then opens on its own — there is nothing to tap. The time is yours to pray in.

How long it holds

Seconds. Five is the least it can be; there is no most.

Your verse over the gate

Proverbs 3:5–6 stays. Beneath it goes a verse of your own — the one that jumped at you. Change it whenever it stops being the one.

Keep & restore

Your days live on this phone only. Nothing is sent anywhere, so nothing can be recovered for you. Keeping a copy is the one habit this asks of you.

Save a file

The plain way. It lands in your downloads with today's date on it, and your phone already backs up files without being asked. Open it in five years and it is still readable, with or without this app.

Or a code

Shorter, for moving straight onto another phone. Paste it into a note or email it to yourself.

Restore from a code

This replaces everything now on this phone. Keep a copy first if there is anything here worth keeping.
